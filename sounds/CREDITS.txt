Rake It Back: where the bird calls came from

The game makes up nearly every sound it plays as it goes. These are the exception: real recordings, each file one
call cut out of the recordings below, filtered, and brought to the same loudness (the loudest 200 ms of each at
about -20 LUFS). The same list is on the pause screen (Esc), under Credits.

Australian brush-turkey (Alectura lathami): brushturkey-1.mp3 to brushturkey-9.mp3
  Source recording: Nick Talbot, XC694790. Accessible at www.xeno-canto.org/694790.
  Recorded 2021-12-29, Tweed Heads South, NSW, Australia.
  Licence: Creative Commons (see the recording page above for the exact licence terms).
  Changes: individual honks were excerpted, band-pass filtered, noise-reduced and rearranged into short calls
  (brushturkey-6 to -9 pitch-shifted lower), then cut into single honks and levelled.

Australian brush-turkey, booming: boom-1.mp3
  Source: the video "Strange call of the Australian Brush-turkey" on YouTube,
  https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vqgRREcKruM (the call about 8 seconds in).
  Licence: not confirmed yet (see the video's page for who posted it and on what terms).
  Changes: the call excerpted, the background noise reduced, band-pass filtered (0.15 to 2.5 kHz) and levelled.
  The game plays it a touch faster or slower each time.

Silver gull (Chroicocephalus novaehollandiae): silvergull-1.mp3 to silvergull-4.mp3
  Source recording: Drew Davison, XC608451. Accessible at www.xeno-canto.org/608451.
  Recorded 2020-12-08, Denmark, Western Australia.
  Licence: Creative Commons (see the recording page above for the exact licence terms).
  Changes: single calls excerpted, band-pass filtered (1 to 7.5 kHz) and levelled.

Galah (Eolophus roseicapilla): galah-1.mp3 to galah-4.mp3
  Source recording: Romuald Mikusek, XC1166610. Accessible at www.xeno-canto.org/1166610.
  Recorded 2023-12-19, Wilsons Promontory, Victoria, Australia.
  Licence: CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/). These clips are shared under
  the same licence.
  Changes: single calls excerpted, band-pass filtered (0.6 to 11 kHz) and levelled.

Cockatoos (sulphur-crested, by the sound of them): cockatoo-1.mp3 to cockatoo-5.mp3, and cockatoos-1.mp3
  Source: "Raucous Cockatoos" by freesound_community on Pixabay,
  https://pixabay.com/sound-effects/raucous-cockatoos-34123/
  Licence: Pixabay Content License (https://pixabay.com/service/license-summary/).
  Changes: single calls (and, in cockatoos-1, a few seconds of the whole mob) excerpted, band-pass filtered
  (0.45 to 10 kHz) and levelled.
